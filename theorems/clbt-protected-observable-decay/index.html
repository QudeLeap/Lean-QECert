CLBT protected-observable decay theorem

Theorem statement

Consider a finite-dimensional quantum system with Hamiltonian $H_{\mathrm{sys}}$. At inverse temperature $\beta$, define the normalized Gibbs state $\rho_\beta=\frac{\exp(-\beta H_{\mathrm{sys}})}{\operatorname{Tr}(\exp(-\beta H_{\mathrm{sys}}))}$. Let $\mathcal{L}$ be an arbitrary jump-form Lindblad dissipator with jump operators $J_a$ and assume $\mathcal{L}(\rho_\beta)=0$. Let $\widetilde{X}$ and $\widetilde{Z}$ be self-adjoint observables satisfying $\widetilde{X}^2=\widetilde{Z}^2=I$ and $\widetilde{X}\widetilde{Z}=-\widetilde{Z}\widetilde{X}$. Let $P$ be an invariant projector satisfying $P\widetilde{X}=\widetilde{X}P$, $P\widetilde{Z}=\widetilde{Z}P$, and, for every jump operator $J_a$, $[J_a,\widetilde{X}]P=[J_a,\widetilde{Z}]P=0$. Assume $\widetilde{X}$, $\widetilde{Z}$, and $P$ commute with $H_{\mathrm{sys}}$, and define $\Phi_t=\exp(-it[H_{\mathrm{sys}},\cdot]+t\mathcal{L})$. Then there exist TPCP maps $\operatorname{Enc}$ and $\operatorname{Dec}$ such that, for every one-qubit state $\eta$ and every $t\ge 0$, $\operatorname{Dec}(\operatorname{Enc}(\eta))=\eta$ and $\|\Phi_t(\operatorname{Enc}(\eta))-\operatorname{Enc}(\eta)\|_1\le 8t\|\mathcal{L}\|_1\operatorname{Tr}((I-P)\rho_\beta)$, where the norm of $\mathcal{L}$ is induced by the trace norm over self-adjoint inputs.
