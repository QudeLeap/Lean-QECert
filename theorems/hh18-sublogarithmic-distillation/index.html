HH18 sublogarithmic magic-state distillation

Theorem statement

Define $h_2(p)=\operatorname{binEntropy}(p)/\log 2$ for $0<p<1$ and $\Gamma(p)=3(1-h_2(p))/h_2(3p)$ for $1/6<p<1/3$. Let $\gamma_{\mathrm{HH}}$ be the infimum, equivalently the greatest lower bound, of $\Gamma(p)$ on that interval. For every $\eta>0$ there are a rational $p$ with $1/6<p<1/3$ and a denominator-clearing subsequence of natural-number triples $(m,r,w)$ satisfying $m=3r+1$, $w=3rp$, and $0\le 2w<2r<m$. For $n=\sum_{i=w+1}^{m}\operatorname{choose}(m,i)$, $k=\sum_{i=0}^{w}\operatorname{choose}(m,i)$, and $d=\sum_{i=w+1}^{r+1}\operatorname{choose}(r+1,i)$, the associated punctured Reed--Muller CSS code has parameters $[[n,k,d]]$ and physical $T$ implements logical $T^{\dagger}$ on every logical qubit; equivalently, physical $T^{\dagger}$ implements logical $T$. There is an actual concatenated magic-state-distillation protocol family whose exponent $\gamma=\log(n/k)/\log d$ satisfies $|\gamma-\gamma_{\mathrm{HH}}|<\eta$ and $\gamma<1$, and whose noisy-input cost per successful output, including success probability, is bounded by a constant multiple of $\log(1/\epsilon)^\gamma$ as $\epsilon\to0$. With $p_0=270629/10^6$, the exact certificate is $\gamma_{\mathrm{HH}}\le\Gamma(p_0)<339/500<1$. This is a sublogarithmic polylogarithmic-overhead result, not a constant-overhead claim.
