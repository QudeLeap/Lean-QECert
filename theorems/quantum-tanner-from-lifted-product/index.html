Quantum Tanner code from selected lifted-product stabilizers

Theorem statement

Let $G$ be a finite group and let $A,B\subseteq G$ be symmetric subsets with $|A|=|B|=\Delta$. In the binary lifted-product construction of the cited source, take full-row-rank parity-check matrices $h_A,h_B$ with $m$ rows and $\Delta$ columns, and dual parity-check matrices $g_A,g_B$ for $(\ker h_A)^\perp$ and $(\ker h_B)^\perp$. Set $C_A=\ker g_A$ and $C_B=\ker h_B$, so $C_A^\perp=\ker h_A$ and $C_B^\perp=\ker g_B$. Use canonical left and right regular actions on the group basis, take the transpose for the X-check boundary, and read the retained support matrices with the induced inverse-column twists while retaining the source's auxiliary blocks. Restrict to the two stabilizer families parametrized by these dual matrices in the displayed bridge construction, and discard the auxiliary qubits outside $G\times A\times B$. Under that construction's square-coordinate identification, the resulting binary CSS check spaces are exactly the Quantum Tanner check spaces with local tensor codes $C_A\otimes C_B$ and $C_A^\perp\otimes C_B^\perp$, using the X/Z naming of the displayed bridge formulas. This is a deterministic identification of this selected construction, not an equivalence of arbitrary lifted-product and Quantum Tanner codes.
