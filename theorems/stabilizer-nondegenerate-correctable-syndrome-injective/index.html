Bounded nondegenerate correctable-error syndrome injectivity

Theorem statement

Let $S$ be a source-valid binary stabilizer group, let $T$ be its exact common-fixed code, and let $M$ be a finite $J$-indexed binary Pauli generator family whose generated stabilizer is exactly $S$. Suppose $T$ has finite semantic Pauli distance $d$ and is nondegenerate at $d$. Let $K_i=a_iE_i$, $i\in I$, be a complete finite binary-Pauli Kraus family for which there exists a CPTP recovery channel correcting $T$, and let $I_+=\{i\in I\mid a_i\ne 0\}$ be its active support. Assume $\operatorname{wt}(E_i)\le t$ for every $i\in I_+$ and $2t<d$. Then, for all $i,j\in I_+$, $f_M(E_i)=f_M(E_j)\Longrightarrow E_i\sim_{\pm}E_j$. Thus the generator-indexed syndrome is injective on the active physical binary-Pauli errors modulo stored global sign. If the chosen active representatives have no duplicates modulo stored sign, then $i\mapsto f_M(E_i)$ is injective on $I_+$.
