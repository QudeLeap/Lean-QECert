KT19 prime-qudit distillation with a vanishing exponent

Theorem statement

Let $p\ge5$ be prime. The qudit Hilbert space is $H_p=\mathbb{C}^p$, and the relevant diagonal third-level gate is $U_{1,3}$. Let $V$ satisfy the strong cubic condition $V*V\subseteq V^\perp$. For natural parameters $l,k$ satisfying $1\le k$, $l\le p$, $3l\le p+1$, $k\le l$, and $2\le l-k$, the Reed--Solomon construction has length $n=p-k$, logical dimension $k$, and proved source-used distance guarantee $d\ge l-k$, with physical $U_{1,3}^{\otimes(p-k)}$ implementing the inverse logical $U_{1,3}^{-1}$ action on the $k$ logical qudits. Its exponent is $\gamma_{p,l,k}=\log((p-k)/k)/\log(l-k)$. For each fixed admissible $p,l,k$, there is a depth-indexed $p$-dependent resource state $M_p$ and actual qudit distillation protocol whose reached error tends to zero as depth grows, equivalently attaining every sufficiently small positive target $\epsilon$, and whose successful-output cost is success-adjusted with exponent $\gamma_{p,l,k}$. Separately, as $p$ varies along a changing-prime sequence, the exponents obey $\gamma_{p,l,k}=O(1/\log p)\to0$; this is not a fixed-alphabet or fixed-resource statement.
