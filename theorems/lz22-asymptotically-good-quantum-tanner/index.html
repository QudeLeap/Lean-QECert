Quantum LDPC / Theorem

Conditional LZ22 asymptotically good Quantum Tanner codes

Theorem statement

Fix real parameters $\rho,\epsilon,\delta$ with $0<\rho<1/2$, $0<\epsilon<1/2$, $0<\delta<1/2$, and $-\delta\log_2\delta-(1-\delta)\log_2(1-\delta)<\rho$. Let $\Delta$ be a sufficiently large fixed degree in the selected odd-prime-degree arithmetic PSL2 subsequence of LZ22. Assume, as an external spectral premise, that on each actual left and right Cayley graph of this family the complex adjacency operator restricted to zero-sum functions has operator norm at most $2\sqrt{\Delta}$. Put $r=\lfloor\rho\Delta\rfloor$. Choose the binary local code $C_A\subseteq\mathbb{F}_2^\Delta$ as the row span of a uniformly random $r\times\Delta$ generator matrix and $C_B\subseteq\mathbb{F}_2^\Delta$ as the kernel of a uniformly random $r\times\Delta$ parity-check matrix. With nonzero probability, fixing this one pair of local codes on the selected infinite family $X$ of TNC left-right Cayley square complexes yields a family $Q$ with arbitrarily large block lengths of binary Quantum Tanner CSS codes. Every member has block length $n$, encoded dimension $k$, and minimum distance $d$ satisfying $k\ge(1-2\rho)^2n$ and $d\ge\delta n/(4\Delta^{3/2+\epsilon})$. Every selected stabilizer generator has weight at most $\Delta^2$, and every qubit belongs to at most $\Delta^2$ selected generators, so this is a quantum LDPC family because $\Delta$ is fixed before the family grows. The probability is only over the local-code matrices chosen before the infinite family; no deterministic or explicit successful choice is asserted.
