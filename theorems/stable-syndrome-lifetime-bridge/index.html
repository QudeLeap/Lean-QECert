Stable-syndrome quantitative storage bridge

Theorem statement

Let $S$ be a stabilizer group with deterministic Pauli correction $C$ on each syndrome $s$, and let $s_E$ be the syndrome of an elementary Pauli error $E$. Call $s$ good when $C(s+s_E)EC(s)\in S$ for every selected elementary error, and let $P_{\mathrm{good}}$ project onto the good syndrome sectors. Let $\widetilde{X}$ and $\widetilde{Z}$ be the decoder-dressed logical observables determined by $C$ from a bare logical Pauli pair, and let $H_{\mathrm{sys}}$ be a local stabilizer Hamiltonian generated by elements of $S$. At inverse temperature $\beta$, define $\rho_\beta=\frac{\exp(-\beta H_{\mathrm{sys}})}{\operatorname{Tr}(\exp(-\beta H_{\mathrm{sys}}))}$. Let $\mathcal{L}$ be a jump-form Lindblad dissipator with jump operators $J_a$, each chosen from the selected elementary Pauli errors, and assume $\mathcal{L}(\rho_\beta)=0$. Then $P_{\mathrm{good}}$ commutes with $H_{\mathrm{sys}}$; the observables $\widetilde{X}$ and $\widetilde{Z}$ are self-adjoint, satisfy $\widetilde{X}^2=\widetilde{Z}^2=I$ and $\widetilde{X}\widetilde{Z}=-\widetilde{Z}\widetilde{X}$, commute with $P_{\mathrm{good}}$ and $H_{\mathrm{sys}}$, and satisfy $[J_a,\widetilde{X}]P_{\mathrm{good}}=[J_a,\widetilde{Z}]P_{\mathrm{good}}=0$ for every jump operator $J_a$. Let $\epsilon_{\mathrm{bad}}=\operatorname{Tr}((I-P_{\mathrm{good}})\rho_\beta)$, equivalently the bad-syndrome partition-weight ratio, and define $\Phi_t=\exp(-it[H_{\mathrm{sys}},\cdot]+t\mathcal{L})$. Consequently, there exist TPCP maps $\operatorname{Enc}$ and $\operatorname{Dec}$ with $\operatorname{Dec}(\operatorname{Enc}(\eta))=\eta$ for every one-qubit state $\eta$. For every one-qubit state $\eta$, every $\delta_{\mathrm{mem}}\ge 0$, and every $t\ge 0$ satisfying $8t\|\mathcal{L}\|_1\epsilon_{\mathrm{bad}}\le \delta_{\mathrm{mem}}$, both $\|\Phi_t(\operatorname{Enc}(\eta))-\operatorname{Enc}(\eta)\|_1\le \delta_{\mathrm{mem}}$ and $\|\operatorname{Dec}(\Phi_t(\operatorname{Enc}(\eta)))-\eta\|_1\le \delta_{\mathrm{mem}}$. If $\|\mathcal{L}\|_1\epsilon_{\mathrm{bad}}=0$, the decoded guarantee holds for every one-qubit state $\eta$ and every $t\ge 0$.
